# ClassDesk business workflow

ClassDesk is an offline pilot for small academies and tuition centres. It stores students, class sections, teachers, attendance and monthly fee records on one computer.

## Enrollment

Add teachers and class sections first. Enter subject, teacher and schedule notes. Enroll students with unique admission numbers, guardian/telephone details and one class reference. Deactivate the Currently enrolled checkbox for leavers rather than reusing their admission number.

## Fee due to receipt

1. Create a Fee due for each student/month with a unique slip reference, due date and net amount due.
2. Always enter the period in YYYY-MM format, for example 2026-10. A student/period pair cannot be duplicated when entered consistently.
3. Reference class fees are not copied automatically; enter the student's agreed net amount and explain any concession in Notes.
4. Record the initial receipt only once. Use Collect payment for later installments. Print the fee slip/record as needed.
5. Due, Part-paid and Settled are calculated from the real balance and receipts. A record becomes Settled when its full amount has been collected; these payment labels cannot be set to contradict the ledger.

Teacher payments record money already paid, with month/date/reference. They are spending, not academy revenue. Do not duplicate them in Expenses; use Expenses for other spending. Attendance accepts one record per student/date and allows Present, Absent or Leave.

## Scope and limits

No batch fee generation, late-fee rules, biometric attendance, SMS, exams, certificates, parent portal, multi-course enrollment or payroll calculations are included. Class schedules are notes, not a conflict-checked calendar. Reference salaries and fees do not create transactions. Fee-month formatting is a user convention. Cancel only unpaid fee records; refunds need an external process. Validate fee reconciliation, printer output and backup recovery in a real pilot before relying on it operationally.

Initial payments explicitly select cash, card or bank. This choice belongs to the receipt created with the record; later collections choose their own method and do not rewrite earlier payment entries.
